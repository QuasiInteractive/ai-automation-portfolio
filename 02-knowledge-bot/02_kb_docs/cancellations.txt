Coastline Cooling - Booking changes and cancellations

You can move or cancel a booking for free up to 24 hours before the slot.
Cancelling with less than 24 hours notice costs $60, unless it is because of illness or bad weather.
If we arrive and cannot get in, the $120 call-out still applies.
If we have to cancel because of weather or an emergency, we will text you and rebook you at the first free slot at no cost.
Deposits: installs over $2,500 need a 20% deposit to book. The deposit is refunded in full if you cancel more than 24 hours before.
