Coastline Cooling - Where we work and when

We cover the Gold Coast from Coomera in the north to Coolangatta in the south, including Helensvale, Southport, Robina, Burleigh Heads, Nerang and Mudgeeraba.
Tamborine Mountain and Beenleigh are covered for installs only, with a $60 travel fee.
We do not work in Brisbane or northern New South Wales.

Business hours are 7am to 4pm, Monday to Friday. Saturday morning installs are available by arrangement.
After-hours repairs are available until 8pm on weekdays and from 8am to 2pm on Saturdays. We do not work on Sundays or public holidays.

Bookings are in two slots: morning (7am to 11am) and arvo (12pm to 4pm). We text when we are on our way.
