Coastline Cooling - Warranty and guarantees

Workmanship: every install carries a 5 year workmanship warranty from us. If something we installed fails because of how we installed it, we fix it free.
Manufacturer warranty: Daikin, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries and Fujitsu units carry a 5 year manufacturer warranty on parts. Compressors are covered for 5 years.
To keep the warranty valid, the unit should be serviced at least once every 12 months. We send a reminder text when a service is due.
Repairs carry a 90 day warranty on the parts and labour for that repair.
The warranty does not cover damage from storms, power surges, pests nesting in the outdoor unit, or work done by someone else.
