Coastline Cooling - Common questions

Which brands do you install? We install Daikin, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries and Fujitsu. We can install a unit you bought yourself, but our workmanship warranty then only covers the install, not the unit.
How long does an install take? A standard single install takes 3 to 4 hours. Back-to-back or double storey installs can take a full day.
Do I need to be home? Someone over 18 needs to be home at the start and end of the job.
How do I pay? We send the bill by text after the job. You can pay by bank transfer or card. Payment is due within 7 days.
Do you charge for quotes? Quotes for installs are free. We can usually quote from photos of the room and the outside wall.
Are you licensed? Yes. We hold an ARCtick refrigerant handling licence and a Queensland electrical contractor licence.
Can you install a ducted system? No, we only install and service split systems.
Do you offer finance? No, we do not offer finance or payment plans.
What size unit do I need? As a rough guide: a bedroom needs 2.5 kW, a small living room 3.5 kW, an open-plan living area 5 to 7 kW. We confirm the size when we quote.
